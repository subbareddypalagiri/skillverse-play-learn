## 🎓 NPTEL Platform Integration

### What is NPTEL?

**NPTEL** = National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning

- **Joint Initiative**: IITs + IISc Bangalore
- **Funded By**: Government of India (MHRD)
- **Certificates**: Paid exam, free learning
- **Recognition**: Industry-wide acceptance

### NPTEL Features:
- ✅ 700+ courses from IITs
- ✅ Video lectures (HD quality)
- ✅ Downloadable transcripts
- ✅ Assignments & quizzes
- ✅ Discussion forums
- ✅ Certificate exam option
