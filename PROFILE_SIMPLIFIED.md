## 🎯 **Design Principles Applied**

### **1. Clarity First** 👁️
- Name is HUGE and BLACK
- Easy to read
- No eye strain

### **2. Clean Borders** 🔲
- Activity cards use borders instead of backgrounds
- Hover effect on borders (energetic!)
- Much cleaner look

### **3. Consistent Colors** 🎨
- Icons keep their colors (red, blue, green, etc.)
- Numbers and text are solid
- Less overwhelming

### **4. Energetic But Simple** ⚡
- Still has fire emoji 🔥
- Still has icons
- Still shows energy
- Just cleaner!
